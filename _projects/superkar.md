SuperKar+ is a 32 voice eurorack voice module. It is able to synthesize realistic string sounds using s Karplus-Strong synthesis algorithm.

I designed and implemented the firmware for this product and worked on the overall system design. SuperKar+ implements a brand new architecture and carries unique challenges relative to Strymon's other products.
